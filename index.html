Interpretation boundary
This prototype summarizes browser camera and microphone signals plus outputs from face-api.js. Expression labels are model probabilities, not verified emotions. The horizontal landmark-ratio proxy is not eye tracking or validated gaze estimation. Pitch and level variation do not establish stress, deception, intent, personality, cognition, mental state, or truthfulness. The -45 dBFS cutoff and 60-350 Hz summary range are engineering choices, not psychological or clinical thresholds. Multi-face frames are excluded from participant-level camera summaries because this prototype does not track identity. No score produced here is validated for clinical, legal, employment, security, or psychological decision making. Captured camera and microphone content is processed in the current browser session and is not uploaded by this page; application scripts and model assets are fetched from external hosts.
